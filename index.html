1. Information We Do Not Collect

StudyPulse works without any user registration, login, or backend server. Your study timer sessions, exam targets, syllabus tasks, revision notes, flashcards, and recent PDF list are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We never collect, transmit, or sell your personal study content.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

StudyPulse uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and optional rewarded video ads (which unlock extra study slots). To serve ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AD_ID), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction metrics.

For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

3. User Choices & Ad Opt-Out

You can limit or opt out of personalized advertising at any time from your Android phone via: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

4. App Permissions & Device Features

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & AD_ID: Required solely by Google AdMob to load banner and rewarded ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to keep the study focus timer running accurately in the notification bar and send optional daily study reminders.
  • Document Picker (Android SAF): Used only when you manually choose a PDF file to view. StudyPulse does not scan or access other files on your phone.
  • Speech Input (Optional Voice-to-Note): Uses your phone's built-in speech recognizer only when you tap the mic icon; StudyPulse does not record or store audio files.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your notes, tasks, and PDF history remain on your device only until you delete them in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall StudyPulse. AdMob diagnostic data is handled according to Google's retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

StudyPulse is designed for general students (IARC 3+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when adding new features or compliance updates. For any privacy questions or support, email us at pyushkushwah531@gmail.com or samratvikash86@gmail.com.
