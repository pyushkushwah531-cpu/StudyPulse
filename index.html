Privacy Policy for StudyPulse

Effective Date: November 2026

StudyPulse is an offline-first student productivity and study companion application developed by Pyush Kushwah.

1. Data Collection and Storage

StudyPulse does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data on external servers. All study timers, exam dates, tasks, flashcards, mistake notes, and recent PDF references are stored locally on your device.

2. Permissions Used

  • Notifications & Foreground Service: Used only to display your active focus timer and send optional daily study reminders.
  • Vibration: Used to alert you when a study focus session completes.
  • Document Access (Storage Access Framework): Used only when you manually select a PDF file to read inside the app.
