Privacy Policy for StudyPulse

Package Name: com.pyush.studypulse
Developer: Heera Lal
Effective Date: November 2026

StudyPulse is an offline-first student productivity, study timer, PDF reader, and revision companion app. Your privacy is fully respected.

1. Data Collection and Storage

StudyPulse does not collect, upload, track, sell, or share any personal or usage data on external servers. All study sessions, exam countdowns, syllabus tasks, notes, flashcards, mistakes, and recent PDF references remain stored strictly locally on your device.

2. Permissions and Why They Are Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to show your active focus timer and send optional daily study reminders.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & SPECIAL_USE): Keeps your active focus timer and optional offline ambient study sound running reliably when the screen is locked.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to reschedule your optional daily study reminder after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Alerts you gently when a focus or break timer finishes.
  • Document Access & File Sharing: Used only when you manually pick a PDF to read or choose to export/share your notes as a PDF.
  • Voice Input: When you tap the microphone icon, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognizer to type notes; StudyPulse does not record or store audio.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Advertising

StudyPulse currently contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or external user tracking libraries.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you have full control. You can delete individual tasks, notes, or recent PDF items inside the app at any time, or erase all app data permanently by clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

StudyPulse is safe for students of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children or adults.
