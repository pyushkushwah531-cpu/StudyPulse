Privacy Policy for StudyPulse - Focus, PDF & Notes

App Package: com.pyush.studypulse

1. Local Study Data (On-Device Only)

StudyPulse does not require any account or login. All your study notes, flashcards, exam tasks, focus timer history, and recent PDF list are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal study data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

StudyPulse uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and optional rewarded video ads (which unlock additional slots beyond the free tier). Google AdMob may automatically collect device information such as the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

3. App Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / AD_ID: Used solely by Google AdMob to load banner and rewarded advertisements.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / VIBRATE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to display the live study timer notification and optional daily study reminders.
  • Document Picker (SAF): Used only when you manually select a PDF file from your phone to read.

4. Children's Privacy & Data Deletion

StudyPulse is rated 3+ and suitable for students. Since no personal study data is stored on external servers, you can permanently delete all app data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling StudyPulse.
